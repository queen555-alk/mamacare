title: Tetanus vaccine in pregnancy
source: WHO guidance on tetanus toxoid vaccination in pregnancy (verify against current national schedule)
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
A tetanus vaccine given during pregnancy protects both the mother and the newborn baby from tetanus, a serious infection that can enter through the umbilical cord or a wound during birth. How many doses you need depends on the vaccines you have received before, so take your vaccination card to your antenatal visit. The health worker will tell you which dose you need and when. Mild soreness at the injection site for a day or two is common.
