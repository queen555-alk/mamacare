title: Antenatal care visits
source: WHO recommendations on antenatal care for a positive pregnancy experience (2016)
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
The World Health Organization recommends a minimum of eight antenatal contacts with a health worker during pregnancy. The first contact should happen early, within the first 12 weeks. After that, visits are recommended at about 20, 26, 30, 34, 36, 38 and 40 weeks of pregnancy. At each visit the health worker checks blood pressure, weight, the growth and heartbeat of the baby, and may do tests and give supplements, vaccines and advice. Starting early and attending every visit helps find problems before they become dangerous. Do not wait for the next appointment if you notice a danger sign.
