title: Swelling and high blood pressure in pregnancy
source: WHO recommendations on antenatal care (2016); general obstetric guidance
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
Mild swelling of the feet and ankles is common in late pregnancy, especially at the end of the day or in hot weather. Resting with your feet raised and avoiding standing for very long can help. However, swelling that appears suddenly, or swelling of the face and hands, especially together with a severe headache, blurred vision or pain in the upper abdomen, can be a sign of pre-eclampsia, a serious condition linked to high blood pressure. This needs urgent care at a health facility. This is one reason your blood pressure is checked at every antenatal visit.
