title: Newborn care and breastfeeding
source: WHO recommendations on newborn care and infant feeding (verify)
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
Start breastfeeding within the first hour after birth if you and the baby are well. Breastfeed exclusively for the first six months, meaning only breast milk with no water, other milk or food, unless a health worker prescribes otherwise. Keep the baby warm, with skin-to-skin contact, and keep the umbilical cord clean and dry. Take the baby to a health facility urgently if the baby is not feeding well, has convulsions, breathes fast or with difficulty, has a fever or feels very cold, is very sleepy or floppy, or has yellow skin in the first days.
