Routine vaccination of a baby starts at birth and continues through infancy: first doses are given at birth, then at about 6, 10 and 14 weeks, again at about 9 months, and later in the second year. The exact vaccines and ages follow the current national schedule, so use your child's immunisation card and ask your health worker. Keep the card safe and bring it to every visit. A mild fever, a little fussiness or soreness where the injection was given are common for a day or two after vaccines. If your child has missed a vaccine, take the child to the health centre, because most missed doses can still be given.
