title: Danger signs in pregnancy
source: WHO pregnancy danger signs and Nigerian antenatal care guidance (verify against current documents)
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
Go to the nearest health facility immediately if you have any of these danger signs during pregnancy: vaginal bleeding, severe headache, blurred vision, convulsions or fits, high fever, severe abdominal pain, fluid leaking from the vagina before labour, the baby moving much less or not at all, difficulty breathing, or sudden swelling of the face and hands. These signs can mean a serious problem for the mother or the baby. Do not wait for your next antenatal appointment and do not try to treat these signs at home. If you cannot get to a facility quickly, ask someone to help you travel and call the local emergency number.
