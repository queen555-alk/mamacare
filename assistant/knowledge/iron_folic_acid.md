title: Iron and folic acid supplements
source: WHO recommendations on antenatal care (2016)
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
The World Health Organization recommends that pregnant women take daily oral iron and folic acid supplements. Iron helps prevent anaemia, which makes mothers tired and can raise risks at delivery. Folic acid helps prevent serious birth defects of the baby's brain and spine, and it matters most before pregnancy and in the early weeks. Take the tablets exactly as your health worker or pharmacist advises and do not take more than prescribed. Iron can cause dark stools or constipation, which is usually harmless. Drinking water and eating fruits and vegetables can help with constipation.
