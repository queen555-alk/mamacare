title: Healthy eating in pregnancy
source: WHO recommendations on antenatal care (2016), nutrition interventions
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
Eat a variety of foods every day: grains and tubers, beans and nuts, eggs, fish or meat, vegetables and fruits. Eat a little more in the later months of pregnancy, and drink plenty of clean, safe water. Avoid alcohol and tobacco completely. Avoid raw or undercooked meat, fish and eggs, and unpasteurised milk. Limit drinks with a lot of caffeine such as strong tea, coffee and some energy drinks. If you often feel very tired, dizzy or pale, tell your health worker, because you may need a check for anaemia.
