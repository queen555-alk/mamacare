title: Preventing malaria in pregnancy
source: WHO guidance on malaria in pregnancy (verify against current Nigerian national malaria guidance)
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
Malaria in pregnancy is dangerous for both the mother and the baby. Sleep under an insecticide-treated mosquito net every night. In areas with malaria, health workers give a preventive treatment during antenatal visits, starting in the second trimester, with doses at least one month apart. Ask your health worker about it at your visits. If you have a fever or feel unwell with chills, go to a health facility to be tested. Do not treat suspected malaria yourself with leftover or bought drugs, because some medicines are not safe in pregnancy.
