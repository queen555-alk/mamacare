title: Baby movements in pregnancy
source: General antenatal guidance, consistent with WHO recommendations (verify)
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
Most women first feel their baby move between about 16 and 25 weeks of pregnancy. First-time mothers often feel it a little later. Movements feel like flutters, kicks or rolling. In the later months you should notice your baby moving regularly. If your baby is moving much less than usual or you have not felt any movement, do not wait. Go to a health facility the same day to have the baby checked. Do not rely on drinks, food or home methods to make the baby move.
