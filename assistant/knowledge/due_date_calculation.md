title: How the due date is calculated
source: Standard obstetric practice (Naegele's rule); an early ultrasound scan can refine the date
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
The expected date of delivery is usually worked out from the first day of the last menstrual period, adding 280 days, which is 40 weeks. This is called Naegele's rule. An ultrasound scan in early pregnancy can give a more accurate date if your periods are irregular or you are unsure of your dates. Only a small share of babies are born on the exact due date. A pregnancy is considered full term from 37 to 42 weeks. Contact a health worker if you pass your due date or have concerns about timing.
