title: Preparing for delivery and signs of labour
source: WHO recommendations on intrapartum care and birth preparedness (verify)
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
Plan for the birth ahead of time. Decide where you will deliver, preferably at a health facility with a trained birth attendant. Arrange transport and some money, choose a companion to go with you, and pack a bag with clothes, a wrapper, and your antenatal card. Learn the signs of labour: regular painful contractions that become stronger and closer together, a bloody mucus discharge, or your water breaking. Go to the facility when labour starts or if your water breaks. Go immediately if there is heavy bleeding, severe headache, convulsions or the baby stops moving.
