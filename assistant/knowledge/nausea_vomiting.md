title: Nausea and vomiting in pregnancy
source: WHO recommendations on antenatal care (2016), management of common physiological symptoms
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
Nausea and vomiting, often called morning sickness, are common in the first months of pregnancy. Eating small meals often, eating a dry snack before getting out of bed, drinking small sips of fluid through the day, avoiding strong smells and resting can help. Ginger is a common remedy that many women find helpful. See a health worker if you cannot keep any fluids down, you vomit many times a day, you lose weight, you feel very weak or dizzy, or your urine becomes very dark or very little. These can be signs of dehydration that need treatment.
