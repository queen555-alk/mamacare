title: Care after birth for mother and baby
source: WHO recommendations on postnatal care of the mother and newborn (verify)
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
After delivery, mother and baby should be checked by a health worker within the first 24 hours and stay at the facility for at least that long if the birth took place there. Further check-ups are recommended at about 3 days, between 7 and 14 days, and at about 6 weeks after birth. Mothers should go to a facility urgently for very heavy bleeding, fever, severe headache, foul-smelling vaginal discharge, severe abdominal pain, convulsions or difficulty breathing. Rest, eat well, drink plenty of water, and tell your health worker if you feel very sad or hopeless for more than a couple of weeks.
