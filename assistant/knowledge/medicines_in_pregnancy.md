title: Medicines and herbal mixtures in pregnancy
source: General safety guidance, consistent with WHO antenatal care recommendations (verify)
status: DRAFT - paraphrased summary, needs clinician review
---
Do not take any medicine during pregnancy without asking a doctor, nurse, midwife or pharmacist who knows you are pregnant. This includes painkillers, antibiotics, malaria drugs, cough mixtures, herbal mixtures and traditional preparations, and drugs bought from roadside sellers. Some medicines and herbs can harm the baby or cause bleeding or early labour. Continue the supplements and treatments your health worker has prescribed. This assistant cannot recommend medicines or doses.
